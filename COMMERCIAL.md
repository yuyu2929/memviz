## Why MIT and not a copyleft license

memviz is a **header you include**. A copyleft license on a header would infect
every file that includes it — nobody would adopt it, and the library would die
of good intentions. MIT keeps adoption free, and support is what companies
actually pay for when a piece of infrastructure ends up in their build.
